# Recover a stuck monitor without restarting the app

Generated by Lazarus Project Assistant.

The phone failed to sync with the desktop several times in the last couple of hours, then stayed offline until the process was killed. Restarting the app restored the conversation. Pairing was still valid. The desktop was still serving that conversation.

While this plan was being written, the phone also stuck on Busy and then did not keep up with the running desktop turn. The link stayed up. That is a separate fault from the offline latch, and it is in section 6.

This is the same recovery shape as the 22:49 UTC incident on 11 November (return from WhatsApp, a toast, then offline while the desktop was up). That incident is in `.assistant/conversation-20261111T233921791.jsonl`. A foreground Wi-Fi refresh was added after it. The later drops still needed a process restart, so that refresh is not sufficient.

No logcat and no desktop `DebugLn` capture from these later drops is in the repository. `LanSession` logs one incident with `android.util.Log.d("MonitorLink", …)`, and that record dies with the process. The diagnosis below is the reported symptom plus the source paths that only a new process clears. It is not a frame-by-frame reconstruction of tonight's drops. Do not invent a log that was not captured.

A new process works because `showCachedThenConnect` calls `WifiLanWatch.start()` and `session.connect`, with `terminal` false and the saved pairing intact. Return to the app, a touch, and Settings Reconnect do not do that when the session has latched.

## 1. Findings

The chip prints Offline when the phase is not an in-use retry. `connectionChip` does that for Paused, for a session that is not `reconnectable`, and for the banners `Offline until the screen is touched`, `Waiting for Wi-Fi`, and `In the background`. A session that is actually backing off prints Reconnecting. "Stuck offline" means one of those parked states, not a healthy 30-second retry.

Three in-process latches match "restart restores the conversation, nothing else does":

- `rejected` other than `busy` becomes `ReadEnd.Rejected`. `runLoop` then calls `policy.pause()`. `auth`, `expired`, `pair_code`, `fingerprint`, `revoked`, and `disabled` also call `markTerminal`. `enterForeground` resets backoff only when the policy is not paused. `mayReconnect` requires `!policy.paused` and `!terminal`. `noteActivity` returns immediately when that is false, so a touch does not wake `resumes.receive()`. Settings Reconnect calls `restartTransient`, and `transientRestartAllowed` refuses a paused or terminal session. A new process does not inherit either flag.
- `bye` reason `replaced` pauses with "Paired elsewhere". `reconnected` retries. `Takeover` sends `replaced` when `FConnectionClientId` is empty or differs. If this process still has the old socket, that pause applies to the whole session, including the socket that just opened.
- `refreshLanRoute` runs from `enterForeground` and from `recoverStaleRoute` after `LanRouteUnavailable` only. `onWifiLost` clears `lanRoute` and does not re-sample or close the live socket. `noteActivity` does not refresh. An unbound socket follows the default route. A desk LAN with no internet is then unreachable, and every retry fails the same way. Process start is what binds the radio again. After two stale skips, `recoverStaleRoute` stops asking for an immediate retry. An open failure publishes the exception text as the banner, and `statusNotice` toasts any banner that is not a known link banner. That was the WhatsApp toast.

`TryAdopt` already returns false with an empty reject when a controller is held, and `AcceptLink` strips the spare `busy` reject. A second socket can become a candidate and authenticate. Do not reopen that admission order. The hole is that the phone then latches, and that a socket which is open but not syncing is not replaced by a touch.

`revoked`, `disabled`, a pin mismatch, and an explicit disconnect must still stay stopped. Do not clear pairing or Room on any of these repairs.

Busy is not freshness. The chip prints Busy when the phase is Live and `statusBusy` is set. That flag is copied from the last status state, and the wire value is `Sending Or PlanRunning`. It stays true for the whole desktop turn. `linkChanged` clears it only on a drop, a pause, or replacement. A phone that has stopped applying the open turn therefore remains Busy for as long as that status frame is the last one it applied.

The live pump and the status push share one IDE-thread pass. `PumpMirror` returns immediately while `FPumping` is set, so a long `ObserveTurn` drops the next tick instead of queueing it. Status that would leave Busy, and the next live delta, wait behind that pass.

The desktop will not queue more than `MonitorCreditWindowBytes` (512 KiB) of unacked log. The phone pauses every socket read while `AccountedEvents` is holding events, and heartbeats still run. The chip stays on the last Busy status. New turn text sits unapplied. `noteCommittedProgress` runs on every applied batch, so a phone that is draining an old window, always behind a turn that is still growing, never goes quiet for `SyncRecoveryBudgetMs`. Recovery does not fire. The open turn never reaches the desktop head. That is the failure to keep up.

A body over `MonitorAppendSliceBytes` (4096) is a deferred replace, not an append. `FetchCap` is 2, and a plan fetch may take the second slot. A resume drops in-flight body replies. A running block can stay a placeholder while the desktop head moves.

## 2. Clear a recoverable latch

### 2.1 Retry a transient reject
Status: complete

In `LanSession.onFrame`, keep `revoked`, `disabled`, `fingerprint`, and `pair_code` as user-action failures: pause, and mark terminal where that already distinguishes them. An already-paired session that receives `auth`, `expired`, `busy`, an empty reason, or `protocol` must back off and try again. Do not call `markTerminal` for those. A truncated close must not be stored as a terminal authentication failure.

Acceptance: a fake host sends `rejected` with `auth` and with `expired` to a paired session. The loop attempts again with no call to `resume` and no new process. `revoked` and `disabled` still pause and do not retry. Cover this in `LanSessionTest`.

### 2.2 Let return, touch, and Reconnect clear that latch
Status: complete

`enterForeground`, `noteActivity`, and `restartTransient` must clear a pause that item 2.1 would not have kept, then start an attempt. Explicit `disconnect()`, `revoked`, `disabled`, and a pin failure stay stopped. Reconnect must not clear pairing or Room.

`transientRestartAllowed` currently hides Settings Reconnect for every paused session. After this item, Reconnect is available for a recoverable pause and for a transient terminal flag. It stays hidden for user disconnect, revocation, disable, and pin failure.

Acceptance: pause the policy as a transient reject would, then call `enterForeground`, `noteActivity`, and `restartTransient` on separate sessions. Each starts a new attempt. After `disconnect()` or `revoked`, all three do nothing. Extend `ConnectionDiagnosticTest` for the button rule.

### 2.3 Do not park on this session's own replacement
Status: complete

`replaced` must retry when this session is opening or holding the replacement, or when the saved client id is the one still in `HostStore`. Show "Paired elsewhere" and pause only when a different client took the desktop and this session did not open that socket. `reconnected` already retries; keep that.

Acceptance: deliver `bye` `replaced` while this session is opening the next socket. The loop retries and does not stay Paused. A `replaced` bye with no local reconnect still shows Paired elsewhere and does not spin. Cover both in `LanSessionTest`.

## 3. Bind the desk LAN without a new process

### 3.1 Re-sample Wi-Fi before every open
Status: complete

Call `refreshLanRoute` at the start of every `attempt`, including a touch-triggered retry while the app stays in the foreground. If the radio is up, remember that bind and set `lanAvailable`. If the saved bind is gone, do not open an unbound socket onto mobile data; sample again or wait for a confirmed absence. Do not publish `LanRouteUnavailable` as the banner, and do not toast it.

Keep the existing `enterForeground` refresh. It is necessary for a process that was frozen. It is not the only call site.

Acceptance: forget the bind, leave the session in the foreground, and recover on the next attempt with no `enterForeground` and no new process. The open uses the desk Wi-Fi even when that network has no internet. No toast contains the route exception. Extend `WifiLanWatchTest` and `LanSessionTest`.

### 3.2 A loss hint must not swallow a due retry
Status: complete

`runLoop` parks on `wakes.receive()` when `!lanAvailable` and nothing is due. A spurious `onLost` that cleared the route then leaves the chip on Offline until a wake that `mayReconnect` accepts. Confirmed absence may still show `Waiting for Wi-Fi`. A due retry, a touch, a foreground return, and a monitoring demand must sample again and open. They must not wait for a callback that already fired.

Acceptance: report loss while the second sample still lists Wi-Fi. The next in-use retry binds that network. A real absence still waits. A touch after that absence samples again instead of staying in `resumes.receive()` or a dead `wakes.receive()`.

## 4. Replace a socket that is up but not syncing

### 4.1 A touch and the sync budget must drop a non-delivering socket
Status: complete

`noteActivity` returns when `link != null`, so a touch cannot replace a socket that is open and not applying events. `onWifiLost` does not close that socket. `forceReconnect` is the applier's recovery, but only when it is reached and the phone does not then latch.

A touch, a foreground return, and the existing sync-recovery budget must close a socket that has had no committed cursor or baseline progress. Ping receipt is not progress. A healthy transfer must not be torn down by a touch. Resume after the new open from the Room cursor. Dedupe that resume on the socket generation, not only the reconnect-loop epoch, so a new socket is not refused the cursor the previous socket already queued.

Acceptance: heartbeats continue and no cursor commits. A touch, and separately the recovery budget, closes that socket. The next open resumes from Room and the conversation updates. A transfer that is committing is not reconnected by a touch. Cover the resume dedupe in the existing sync or session test.

## 6. Follow a running turn while the chip says Busy

### 6.1 Do not let one observation freeze the live pump
Status: complete

`PumpMirror` must not drop the next observation because `FPumping` is still set, and it must not hold the IDE thread across a multi-megabyte diff. Bound the work of one pass. A pass that cannot emit the whole delta must leave a cursor and run again on the next tick. Push a status change even when the turn diff is still in progress, so a finished turn can leave Busy without waiting for the rest of the encode.

Keep unchanged block bodies uncopied. A suffix stays an append. A full replace stays deferred. Do not move this work onto the socket thread.

Acceptance: a live turn that grows by more than one credit window during one pass still emits the remainder on the following tick. A status change during that pass is not delayed until the diff finishes. The IDE thread is not held for the whole diff. Cover the bound in the existing monitor mirror or bridge harness.

### 6.2 Catch up when commits trickle but the open turn is behind
Status: complete

A committed batch is not proof the phone has the desktop head. Track the latest applied sequence for the open turn separately from "any commit". If that sequence is behind the announced head for the recovery budget, or no append or body for the open block has been applied in that budget while the desktop turn is still running, resume from the last durable cursor and show catching up.

Do not leave the chip on Busy for that stall. Busy means the desktop turn is running and the phone's open block is the latest applied head. A stale open block shows catching up, then Busy or Live once it matches. Ping receipt and a trickle of older events must not reset this clock. Do not tear down a transfer that is closing the gap.

Acceptance: generate a running turn faster than the phone applies it. Within the recovery budget the phone either reaches that head or shows catching up and then the head, without a process restart. A transfer that is closing the gap is not reconnected. A finished turn clears Busy when its status frame arrives, even if an older body fetch is still running. Extend the sync applier test and `ConnectionChipTest`.

### 6.3 Keep the open block's body ahead of plan and history fetches
Status: complete

The running block owns a fetch slot. A plan fetch and a history fetch must not take that slot. A newer revision releases the previous fetch and asks for the new body. `applyBlockData` returning Applied must release the slot even if the board watch has not yet republished the block. A resume retries the open body after it drops in-flight replies, instead of leaving the block deferred with no grant.

Acceptance: a running block past 4096 bytes updates on the phone while a plan fetch is also owed. A revision change does not leave the previous fetch in flight. After a resume, the open body is requested again. Cover this in `ScreenFetch` tests.
